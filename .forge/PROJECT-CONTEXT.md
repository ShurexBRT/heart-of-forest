## Product truth
Heart of Forest is in production-polish mode around a Golden Slice. The identity is that Ayla restores the world rather than conquering it. Existing combat, quests, inventory, talents, farming and day-cycle systems are supporting systems, not invitations to expand scope indefinitely.

## Scope rule
Do not add new biomes, broad new systems or genre pivots without an explicit product decision. Prefer work that improves combat feel, restoration payoff, readability, stability and Golden Slice quality.

## Mandatory execution context
Agents must read `AGENTS.md`, `.forge/project.json`, `.forge/agents/<role>.md` and this file. Save compatibility, progression, world-state consequences, Golden Slice verification and the current branch reconciliation risk are mandatory checks.

## Product decision rule
If a proposed change expands the product beyond the frozen polish scope, stop and escalate instead of treating it as ordinary backlog work.
